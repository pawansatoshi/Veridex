WHY VERIDEX

Evidence before interpretation.

Veridex separates what a contract can do from who can exercise that power, and keeps provider failures from becoming false conclusions.

THE EVIDENCE PATH
A capability is only as strong as the evidence behind it.

The analysis follows the actual contract surface: requested address, live code, proxy resolution, verification evidence, capability checks, authority and confidence.
