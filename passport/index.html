Step 3 · Preserve

A compact snapshot of what the contract can do.

A Capability Passport packages one observation: capabilities, evidence fingerprint, verification, confidence and proxy composition. It is not a vulnerability score.

What to do
Enter the same contract you just analyzed, then select Build Passport.

After the Passport is built, use Inspect evidence to understand the finding or Watch to keep the contract in your browser workspace.
